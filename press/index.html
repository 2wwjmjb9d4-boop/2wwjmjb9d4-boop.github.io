About the game

Pixel Pour is a calm, colourful puzzle. Every grain falls, piles and slides like real sand, so dunes, peaks and waves form as you pour. Pick a colour, press to pour and drag to move the spout. Wet Sand stacks straight up for towers and sharp edges, and the Scoop digs out mistakes. A live score shows how close you are; earn three stars and the scene comes to life: boats sail, fish leap, volcanoes erupt and lighthouses blaze.

Features

  • Hand-made scenes, then endless themed chapters: landmarks, seaside, countryside, animals, winter, night skies
  • A new daily picture every day, with seasonal and holiday themes
  • Free Play sandbox and a gallery of your own sand art
  • Original music and sound for every scene, made by the game itself
  • Share a time-lapse video of your pour, or challenge friends with a code
  • Game Center leaderboards (opt-in)
  • No timers, no lives, no energy systems
  • Fair by design: no interstitial ads, nothing random sold, every price shown, bought help never ranked
  • Accessibility: colour patterns, bigger text, less motion and a left-handed layout
